Case 01

VoiceBrew · AI voice agent launch

Made for Blostem1:14 · 16:9After Effects

The job

Launch an AI voice agent that calls customers in Hindi and English, and show what it does in about a minute.

What I did

  • Built a dark, warm look where ringing phones turn into UI cards.
  • Animated the real features: compliance checks, spam-adaptive routing, a live Hindi–English transcript and a lead score.
  • Closed on three proof numbers: 10× more qualified leads, 1/5 the cost of a human team, a live demo call in under 60 seconds.
